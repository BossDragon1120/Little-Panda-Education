**Fact check before final:**
- "Starting to use light", not "switching": light already links server racks; inside a rack, chips still mostly talk over copper; light right next to the chip (co-packaged optics) is early.
- "Often the slowdown isn't the math. It's the traffic between chips": a major bottleneck, not the only one.
- "Light moves more of the data", not all of it.
- "Thousands of chips" is a lower bound; the biggest training runs use tens of thousands or more.
- "Silicon" is shorthand for electronic chips; light parts are often built on silicon too (silicon photonics), left out for a layman.
