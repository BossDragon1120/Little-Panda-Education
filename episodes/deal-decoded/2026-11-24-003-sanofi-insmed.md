**[0:00] Hook**  
Sanofi just agreed to buy Insmed for about 41 billion dollars, all cash. It's a giant bet on your lungs.
*On screen: SNY + INSM · ~$41B · all cash*

**[0:07] 1 · Who's buying: Sanofi**  
Sanofi is a French drug giant. Think of a restaurant chain whose bestselling dish is Dupixent, the eczema and asthma drug from all those ads. But patents run out, and then copycats can cook the same dish.

**[0:20] 2 · Who's being bought: Insmed**  
Insmed is the food truck with a line around the block. Its star dish, Brinsupri, is a daily pill for a chronic lung disease. It launched in August 2025 and has already brought in about 1.09 billion dollars.

**[0:30] 3 · My synergy hypothesis**  
The chain is buying its next bestseller before the old one fades. Sanofi already sells Dupixent for asthma and COPD, so it knows the lung doctors and can take Brinsupri to more countries, faster. Insmed also brings a second approved lung drug and a third in late-stage trials.

**[0:50] 4 · Why this touches your life**  
Picture a relative with a cough that never goes away. Mucus every morning, antibiotics every few months. That's who this drug is for.

**[1:00] 5 · What I'm watching**  
Shareholders and regulators still have to sign off. My integration worry: new drug launches live on momentum. Reshuffle sales teams mid-launch and growth can stall.

**[1:10] 6 · The deal card**  
Here's the deal card. Screenshot it. 175 dollars a share in cash, about 41 billion total. Sanofi expects it to boost earnings per share by the end of 2028, with closing in the first half of 2027. Follow for the next deal, decoded.

## Fact-check notes
- All deal figures match Sanofi's 6-K and Insmed's 8-K (Nov 24, 2026). The ~46% premium comes from Insmed's release (to the Nov 18 unaffected close, the day before media reports). Enterprise value, synergy targets, customer overlap and post-close leadership weren't disclosed, so they're left off the card.
- Brinsupri revenue: release says "$1.09 billion in revenue through September 30, 2026". That's cumulative since the Aug 2025 launch (Insmed's Q3 8-K shows $917.1M for 9M 2026), so the caption's "$1B in barely a year" holds (~14 months).
- Dupixent is Sanofi's bestselling drug (~40% of Q1 2026 sales) and is co-developed with Regeneron. Approved for eczema, asthma and COPD. Key US patent expires March 2031. It's a biologic, so copies are biosimilars; "copycats" is an analogy.
- Third drug = TPIP (treprostinil palmitil), Phase 3, not approved anywhere.
- Ticker SNY (Sanofi's Nasdaq ADR) wasn't named in the documents read; INSM confirmed.
- Opinion / illustrative: restaurant-chain analogy, "knows the lung doctors" sales hypothesis, the "cough that won't go away" scene, launch-momentum concern.
