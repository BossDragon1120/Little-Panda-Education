# Course Explainers

**Subject:** AI, Tech or Business, depending on the course · **Input:** 📚 learning notes
**Premise:** when I finish a course module, the key ideas become a 60–90 second video for people who want the ideas without taking the whole course.
**Where the notes come from:** [Upskilling & Subject Matter Expertise](https://github.com/BossDragon1120/Upskilling-and-Subject-Matter-Expertise). The `learning-notes-intake` agent reads new notes there and hands teachable ideas to `course-explainer-script`.
**Look:** the house standard set by the [`course-explainer-video`](../skills/course-explainer-video) skill: a frosted text panel over a soft dusk gradient, Anton headlines, indigo accent, and one animated diagram per idea, ending with a cheat sheet to screenshot. Every module of a course shares the look. First course: Andrew Ng's Agentic AI (DeepLearning.AI); the Module 5 video (patterns for autonomous agents) set the standard.
**Pipeline status:** Intake ✅ · Script 🛠️ · Slides + video ✅ (`course-explainer-video`) · Assemble 🛠️
